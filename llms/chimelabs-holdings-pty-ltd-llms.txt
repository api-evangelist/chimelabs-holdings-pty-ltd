# Chime Labs

> AI receptionist and voice automation for Australian trades businesses — answer every call, book jobs, and send quotes before you leave the site.

Chime Labs builds an AI-powered phone receptionist purpose-built for plumbers, electricians, HVAC, locksmiths, pest control, cleaners and other service trades. It answers missed calls 24/7, captures job details, books work into ServiceM8 / Simpro / ServiceTitan / AroFlo / Fergus / Acuity / Google Calendar, and turns quote calls into sent quotes automatically.

Founded in 2025 in Sydney, Australia by Alexis Griveau (CEO) and Mathew Pretel — both ex-Google. Backed by 500 Global. Built for Australian English and the local addresses tradies book to. A longer machine-readable digest is available at /llms-full.txt.
